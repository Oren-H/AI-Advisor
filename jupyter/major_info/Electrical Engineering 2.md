Below is the current (2025–2026 bulletin) overview of the B.S. in Electrical Engineering at Columbia Engineering, focused only on major requirements (EE courses, required CS/IEOR/STAT courses for the major, and EE technical electives). For full School Core and SEAS technical core, see the bulletin; they are intentionally omitted here. 

What’s required in the EE major
- EE introductory and core lecture courses with labs: ELEN E1201; ELEN E3201 + ELEN E3081; ELEN E3331 + ELEN E3083; ELEN E3801 + ELEN E3084; CSEE W3827 + ELEN E3082; ELEN E3106; ELEN E3401; ELEN E3701 or CSEE W4119; ELEN E3043; ELEN E3399; ELEN E3390. Labs are 1 pt and normally taken with the paired lecture. citeturn2view0turn11search0turn8search0
- Other required (major) courses outside EE: one probability course (IEOR E3658 or STAT GU4203) and one data structures course (choose one: COMS W3134, COMS W3136, or COMS W3137). citeturn2view0
- Technical electives (≥18 pts): at least 2 courses (≥6 pts) in one EE “depth” area; at least 2 courses (≥6 pts) outside that depth area for breadth; remaining ≥6 pts are other technical electives (3000+ level; see notes). If APMA E2101 is replaced by approved ODE + Linear Algebra (≥6 pts), the total technical electives reduce to 15 pts. citeturn2view0turn7view0

Important implementation details (brief)
- Labs: ELEN E3081/E3084/E3083/E3082 are 1-pt labs paired with their lectures and typically taken the same term. ELEN E3043 is a senior lab taken after most cores. citeturn11search0
- Probability and Data Structures timing: usually in junior year (Semesters 5–6) but allowed any time Semesters 5–8. citeturn2view0
- Technical elective waiver: If APMA E2101 is replaced by MATH UN2030 + (APMA E3101 or MATH UN2010), total tech electives drop from 18 to 15 pts. citeturn2view0

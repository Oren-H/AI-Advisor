The Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering at Columbia University's Fu Foundation School of Engineering and Applied Science requires the completion of specific major courses, typically distributed over the junior and senior years. Below is a structured plan outlining these courses:

**Junior Year**

*Semester 5:*
- ELEN E3201: Circuit Analysis
- ELEN E3202: Signals and Systems
- ELEN E3203: Electromagnetics
- ELEN E3204: Electronic Circuits

*Semester 6:*
- ELEN E3331: Electronic Circuits Lab
- ELEN E3801: Digital Systems
- ELEN E3803: Digital Systems Lab
- ELEN E3701: Introduction to Communication Systems

**Senior Year**

*Semester 7:*
- ELEN E3998: Projects in Electrical Engineering (Capstone Project)
- Technical Elective 1
- Technical Elective 2

*Semester 8:*
- Technical Elective 3
- Technical Elective 4
- Technical Elective 5

In addition to the core courses, students are required to select technical electives to deepen their knowledge in specific areas of electrical engineering. The department offers various depth areas, each comprising a set of associated courses. Students should consult the department's academic advisors or official resources for the most current list of depth areas and their corresponding courses.

Please note that this plan focuses solely on the major-specific courses and does not include the core curriculum or non-technical electives required by the university. For comprehensive degree requirements, students should refer to the official Columbia Engineering Bulletin.

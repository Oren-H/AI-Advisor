Summary (sources: Columbia Engineering bulletin and EE dept. checklist).

Quick overview
- Degree: B.S. in Electrical Engineering (ABET-accredited). Core EE lecture + lab sequence, a senior capstone (E3390), and 18 points of technical electives (depth + breadth + other) are required. Technical-elective rules (6 courses = ≥18 points): at least two (≥6 pts) must be from one EE “depth” area and at least two (≥6 pts) must be outside that depth (breadth). One data‑structures course and a probability course are also required. Capstone and labs
- The capstone design project (ELEN E3390) is required near the end of the program; ELEN E3399 (fall, 1 pt) is the accompanying EE practice / preparation course. Alternatives may be allowed with instructor/department approval in ELEN E3399 (e.g., ELEN E4350, CSEE W4840, or approved project courses), but ELEN E3390 (or approved equivalent) is the standard required senior design.

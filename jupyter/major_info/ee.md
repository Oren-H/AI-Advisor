Below is a concise, major-only guide for the B.S. in Electrical Engineering at Columbia Engineering (SEAS). It omits Columbia College Core and SEAS first/second‑year technical core outside the EE major (e.g., University Writing, Global Core, Literature Humanities, Physics, Chemistry, The Art of Engineering, introductory Calculus/Multivariable/ODE/Linear Algebra, ENGI E1006).

What is required for the EE major
- EE core lectures and labs
  - ELEN E1201 Introduction to Electrical Engineering (with lab component)
  - ELEN E3201 Circuit Analysis + ELEN E3081 Circuit Analysis Lab
  - ELEN E3331 Electronic Circuits + ELEN E3083 Electronic Circuits Lab
  - CSEE W3827 Fundamentals of Computer Systems + ELEN E3082 Digital Systems Lab
  - ELEN E3801 Signals and Systems + ELEN E3084 Signals and Systems Lab
  - ELEN E3106 Solid State Devices and Materials
  - ELEN E3401 Electromagnetics
  - ELEN E3701 Introduction to Communication Systems or CSEE W4119 Computer Networks (choose one)
  - ELEN E3043 Solid State, Microwave, and Fiber Optics Lab
  - ELEN E3399 Electrical Engineering Practice (1 point, taken before capstone)
  - ELEN E3390 EE Senior Design Project (capstone; normally spring of senior year)
- Other required major courses
  - Probability: IEOR E3658 Probability for Engineers or STAT GU4203 Probability Theory (choose one)
  - Data Structures: COMS W3136 Essential Data Structures or COMS W3134 Data Structures in Java or COMS W3137 Honors Data Structures and Algorithms (choose one)
- Technical electives (≥18 points total)
  - Depth: ≥6 points from one depth area (see lists below)
  - Breadth: ≥6 points outside your chosen depth area (3000+ level with significant engineering content)
  - Additional tech electives: remaining points to reach 18 (math/science/engineering 3000+; restrictions apply). Note: If APMA E2101 was replaced by MATH UN2030 (ODEs) plus APMA E3101 or MATH UN2010 (Linear Algebra), total tech‑elective minimum may be reduced by 3 points by departmental policy.

Depth areas and approved EE courses
Pick one area and complete at least two courses (≥6 points) from that area’s list. Course numbers are shown exactly; many titles are self‑explanatory to EE advisors. Use advisor approval for any substitutions.
- Photonics, Solid‑State Devices, and Electromagnetics
  - ELEN E4193, ELEN E4401, ELEN E4411, ELEN E4420, ELEN E4488, ELEN E4944
- Circuits and Electronics
  - ELEN E4215, ELEN E4312, ELEN E4314, EECS E4321, ELEN E4350, ELEN E4361
- Signals and Systems
  - ELEN E4511, ELEN E4720, ELEN E4810, ELEN E4815, ELEN E4830, ELEN E4835, EEME E3601, EEME E4601, EEOR E4650
- Communications and Networking
  - ELEN E3701, CSEE W4119, CSEE W4140, EECS E4764, ELEN E4702, ELEN E4703, COMS W4181
Notes on depth/breadth electives
- Breadth electives must be outside your selected depth area and at the 3000+ level with substantial engineering content (may include other SEAS departments if technical and non‑overlapping). 
- Some courses may appear in more than one area; they can count toward only one requirement.

Typical year‑by‑year plan (major courses only)
Semester 1:
- ELEN E1201 Introduction to Electrical Engineering (may also be taken in Semester 2)

Semester 2:
- (If not taken earlier) ELEN E1201 Introduction to Electrical Engineering

Semester 3:
- ELEN E3201 Circuit Analysis
- ELEN E3081 Circuit Analysis Lab
- ELEN E3801 Signals and Systems
- ELEN E3084 Signals and Systems Lab

Semester 4:
- ELEN E3331 Electronic Circuits
- ELEN E3083 Electronic Circuits Lab
- CSEE W3827 Fundamentals of Computer Systems
- ELEN E3082 Digital Systems Lab

Semester 5:
- ELEN E3106 Solid State Devices and Materials
- IEOR E3658 Probability for Engineers or STAT GU4203 Probability Theory
- COMS W3136 Essential Data Structures or COMS W3134 or COMS W3137
- EE depth technical elective (1 of 2)

Semester 6:
- ELEN E3401 Electromagnetics
- ELEN E3701 Introduction to Communication Systems or CSEE W4119 Computer Networks
- EE breadth technical elective (1 of 2)

Semester 7:
- ELEN E3043 Solid State, Microwave, and Fiber Optics Lab
- ELEN E3399 Electrical Engineering Practice
- EE depth technical elective (2 of 2)
- EE breadth technical elective (2 of 2)

Semester 8:
- ELEN E3390 EE Senior Design Project (Capstone)
- Additional technical elective(s) to reach the 18‑point tech‑elective minimum (if needed)

Important implementation details
- Capstone alternatives: With prior arrangements made in ELEN E3399, one of ELEN E3998, ELEN E4350, ELEN E4998, EECS E4340, or CSEE W4840 may substitute for ELEN E3390 (must include a qualifying culminating design project and presentation).
- Timing flexibility: Probability, Data Structures, and the three categories of technical electives may be taken in Semesters 5–8 as prerequisites/schedules allow.
- Labs should be taken alongside their paired lectures whenever offered (E3081 with E3201; E3084 with E3801; E3083 with E3331; E3082 with W3827).

Below is a concise, up-to-date summary of Columbia College (CC) school‑wide graduation and Core requirements, with timing notes where courses must or are typically taken. Information reflects the 2025–2026 College Bulletin and official CC resources.

Degree and residency
- Credits and GPA: Minimum 124 points and a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 to earn the B.A.
- Semesters in residence: Students are expected to complete the degree in eight full‑time semesters; the final two semesters must be completed in residence (Morningside campus or a Columbia‑sponsored program).
- Major: One approved major is required. Students declare in spring of sophomore year (4th semester); all major courses must be taken for a letter grade with at least C‑ (departmental exceptions may apply for the first course only).

Core Curriculum (what every CC student completes)
Shared Core courses (same content for all students)
- Literature Humanities (HUMA CC1001–CC1002), 2 semesters, 8 points total. When: Fall and spring of the first year (all first‑years are preregistered).
- University Writing (ENGL CC1010), 1 semester, 3 points. When: Must be taken in the first year; half of the class takes it in fall and half in spring.
- Frontiers of Science (SCNC CC1000), 1 semester, 4 points. When: Must be taken in the first year in the term you are not taking University Writing (all first‑years are preregistered in either UW or FroSci each term). FroSci counts toward the Science Requirement (see below).
- Contemporary Civilization (COCI CC1101–CC1102), 2 semesters, 8 points total. When: Required in the sophomore year (year‑long, fall then spring); Lit Hum is considered the prerequisite.
- Art Humanities (HUMA UN1121), 1 semester, 3 points. When: Expected by end of junior year (can be taken earlier).
- Music Humanities (HUMA UN1123), 1 semester, 3 points. When: Expected by end of junior year (can be taken earlier). Some students may qualify for an exemption exam; otherwise, complete the course for a letter grade.

Distribution‑style Core requirements
- Science Requirement: Three courses total, distributed as follows:
  - Science A: Frontiers of Science (fulfilled by SCNC CC1000 in the first year).
  - Science B: One additional course in a Columbia science department (e.g., Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry, Earth & Environmental Sciences, Ecology/Evolution/Environmental Biology, Physics, Psychology) that meets the B list.
  - Science C: One course from the approved C list (which includes many Science B options plus additional approved courses).
  - Notes: At least 3 points per course; letter grade only. A two‑term sequence is not required but may be used to cover B and C. At least one of the three science courses must be taken at Columbia. (Barnard courses generally do not fulfill CC’s science requirement unless explicitly indicated.)
- Global Core Requirement: Two approved courses (letter grade only). When: Any time before graduation; students often take them in sophomore–junior years. Use the Global Core Navigator to find approved offerings each term.
- Foreign Language Requirement: Demonstrate proficiency equivalent to the second term of Intermediate (often labeled “Intermediate II,” typically the fourth college‑term course) in a single language OR place out via departmental placement/AP or other approved exam OR complete an advanced course that lists Intermediate II (e.g., 2102) as a prerequisite. When: Timing is flexible, but you must complete the language requirement before you are eligible to study abroad. All language courses used toward the requirement must be taken in sequence and for a letter grade.

Physical education and swim test
- Physical Education: Two semesters (typically 1 point each), pass/withdrawal grading only. You may take only one PE course per semester; up to 4 PE points may count toward the degree after the two‑term minimum is met. Approved Barnard Dance courses may be used to fulfill PE.
- Swim Test: Required for graduation. Fulfilled by passing the swim test (three continuous laps; 75 yards) or by passing a beginner swim PE course. Strongly encouraged to complete before your final academic semester; medical/religious waivers are available through the appropriate offices.

Grading and other Core rules
- All Core courses (except PE) must be taken for a letter grade; Pass/D/Fail cannot be applied to Core (again, PE is pass/withdrawal only).
- First‑year completion rule: Students are required to complete both semesters of Literature Humanities, Frontiers of Science, and University Writing in the first year; students who do not complete these by year‑end are placed on academic probation.
- Contemporary Civilization must be taken in the sophomore year (following Lit Hum).
- Study abroad and transfer: Some Global Core or Science courses from approved programs may be petitioned for Core credit; at least one of the three science courses must be taken at Columbia.

At‑a‑glance timing guide by semester (typical pattern)
- First year fall: Literature Humanities; either University Writing or Frontiers of Science; plus additional courses (e.g., language, math/science/electives, PE, Global Core).
- First year spring: Literature Humanities; the other course (UW or FroSci) not taken in fall; plus additional courses.
- Sophomore year: Contemporary Civilization I (fall) and II (spring) across the full year; continue/complete language, Science B/C, Global Core, and start/continue the major; consider one PE term if not yet done.
- Junior year: Complete Art Hum and Music Hum by year’s end; finish remaining Core/major requirements; complete swim test if not already done.

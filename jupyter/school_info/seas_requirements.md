First‑Year/Sophomore technical requirements (what you take early and when)
- Calculus: Begin in the first year. Placement determines your entry (MATH UN1101 Calculus I; MATH UN1102 Calculus II; or APMA E2000 Multivariable Calculus). Completion of Multivariable Calculus (APMA E2000) is required for almost all SEAS majors. Typically completed across the first and second years. citeturn5view0
- Physics: Take a full-year introductory sequence in the first year (fall and spring). Options include PHYS CC1401–CC1402; PHYS CC1601–CC1602; or the accelerated PHYS CC2801–CC2802 (by placement/AP). Some majors may require additional physics. citeturn6view0
- Chemistry: At least one semester is required (some majors need two). All incoming students attend the Chemistry information session and placement exam during Orientation to determine the track. First-year tracks include: (1) General Chemistry CHEM UN1403–UN1404 with lab UN1500; (2) Intensive General Chemistry CHEM UN1604 with lab UN1507; or (3) First‑year Organic Chemistry CHEM UN2045–UN2046 with lab UN1507. Liberal Arts Core for Engineering (the 27‑point nontechnical requirement)
- Complete at least 27 points total: 16–18 points from List A (required nontechnical courses) and 9–11 points from List B (approved nontechnical electives). List A courses must be taken at Columbia. Advanced Placement may count toward the elective nontechnical requirement and may satisfy Principles of Economics. citeturn3view0
- List A (required nontechnical courses):
  • University Writing (ENGL CC1010) — complete in the first year.
  • A two‑semester Core Humanities sequence — choose one: Literature Humanities (HUMA CC1001–CC1002), or Contemporary Civilization (COCI CC1101–CC1102), or two approved Global Core courses.
  • One Art Humanities (HUMA UN1121) or Music Humanities (HUMA UN1123).
  • Principles of Economics (ECON UN1105) — AP may satisfy this requirement. Most students complete the Humanities sequence in the second year. citeturn3view0turn4view0

Physical Education
- Two terms of Physical Education are required. No more than 4 PE points may count toward the degree, and students may take at most one PE per term. SEAS students do not have a swimming test requirement; that applies to Columbia College only. You may count certain Barnard dance courses toward PE. Take PE any time before graduation. citeturn0search7turn12search1
